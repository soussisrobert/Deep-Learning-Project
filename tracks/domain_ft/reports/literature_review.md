# HiFT: bounded primary-source review

**Checked 2026-10-23. Status: literature evidence, not reproduced experiments.** Scope: source-resolution-aware hierarchical fusion for single-object UAV tracking. No novelty, accuracy, speed, or checkpoint-reproduction claim is made. Search results were discovery aids; the evidence below is the paper itself, publisher XML, CVF proceedings, or arXiv. PMC links are publisher-deposited full papers, not review summaries. No training or implementation was performed for this review.

## Comparison and evidence

“Not established” means no explicit source-pixel-conditioned hierarchical gate was established from the inspected description; it is **not** proof of absence. Attention may already adapt implicitly to image quality. “Resolution” in different papers need not denote the same variable.

### Additional closest axes, not an exhaustive survey

- **ZoomTrack:** Yutong Kou et al., *ZoomTrack: Target-aware Non-uniform Resizing for Efficient Visual Tracking*, [arXiv:2310.10071, 2023-10-16](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.10071), record states NeurIPS 2023. Target-likely regions retain more raw information under a smaller input budget. This is a particularly relevant **sampling/footprint** comparator, not hierarchical level gating. It cannot recover detail absent in the source.
- **SE-SiamFC:** Ivan Sosnovik, Artem Moskalev, Arnold W. M. Smeulders, *Scale Equivariance Improves Siamese Tracking*, [WACV **2021**, 2765–2774](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/WACV2021/html/Sosnovik_Scale_Equivariance_Improves_Siamese_Tracking_WACV_2021_paper.html). Built-in scale-equivariant similarity models geometric size variation. Scale equivariance is not source-resolution estimation or proof of a source-conditioned fusion rule.
- **ATOM:** Martin Danelljan, Goutam Bhat, Fahad Shahbaz Khan, Michael Felsberg, *ATOM: Accurate Tracking by Overlap Maximization*, [CVPR **2019**, 4660–4669](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_CVPR_2019/html/Danelljan_ATOM_Accurate_Tracking_by_Overlap_Maximization_CVPR_2019_paper.html). Learned overlap estimation separates localization quality from classification. Quality-aware localization predates the recent named methods; a new gate must beat a quality-only explanation.

## Resolution interpretation and open questions

Original-frame target width/height in pixels describe **geometric sampled support**, not guaranteed independent informative pixels: optics, blur, compression, occlusion and earlier resampling matter. Resized target footprint describes how much of the network input the target occupies. Changing one need not change the other. See the derivation and controlled diagnostic in [research hypothesis](../docs/research_hypothesis.md).

**Resolved:** all nine requested names match primary papers and their supplied years. **Unresolved:** LoReTrack's peer-reviewed venue/later publication date; exact day of CVF EATrack publication; code/checkpoint-to-paper correspondence and fair reproduction of every method. This bounded search did **not** establish an exact nearest paper explicitly gating hierarchical UAV features on original source support while holding footprint/quality fixed. That is an open search/evidence gap, **not a novelty conclusion**. Broader scale-adaptive fusion and modality-quality literature still requires systematic screening before positioning any contribution.

AMST² has a [2025-05-26 correction](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12106796/) (DOI 10.1038/s41598-025-00188-y): PDF equation typography/missing tilde and a missing Fig. 3(a) arrow; the notice says the original HTML equations were correct. Use corrected material for mathematical comparison. Do not copy comparative claims about other trackers from a paper's related work without checking those trackers' primary papers.

**Decision:** retain only the conditional, falsifiable question: does source support explain a reproducible change in useful hierarchical evidence **beyond** footprint, crop containment, aspect ratio, motion, visibility and prediction quality? Neither hierarchy, attention, small-target motivation nor quality adaptation alone supports a contribution claim. Published performance numbers were deliberately not pooled: protocols, training data, modalities and hardware differ, and none were reproduced here.
