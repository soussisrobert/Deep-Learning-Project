## 2. Leakage between UAVDark135 and NAT2021
Overlap check, job 49103 (`tools/leak_one.py`, same method as entry 62: thumbnail correlation >= 0.95 and high-pass correlation >= 0.5 against all 194,379 NAT2021 + NAT2021L frames): 0 of 2,578 sampled UAVDark135 frames match NAT2021 or NAT2021L, 0 of 135 sequences; the highest thumbnail correlation among non-matches was 0.914 (below the 0.95 threshold, no borderline cases). A dHash check (entry 57) was discarded because it collides on dark frames. In contrast DarkTrack2021 overlaps NAT2021 (100 of 110 sequences) and was dropped from training. Inside UAVDark135, 14 sequence pairs share footage with each other; the train / val / test split was made by whole linked group (entry 64) so no linked pair straddles two splits. Limits: the check samples frames (first and every 50th), the negative control is weak (entry 62), and no later re-check was run. **GAP: a frame-level check of UAVDark135 train / val / test against each other after the split was not found in the logbook.** NAT2021L itself overlaps NAT2021 (13 of 23 videos start from the same first box as a NAT2021 video, E9).
