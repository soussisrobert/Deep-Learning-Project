4. **Observed integration failures during concurrent source edits, not waived:** actual guarded CLI runs rejected changed bound source bytes while teammates edited them. These failures demonstrate source closure enforcement; they are not successful regression evidence. A final quiescent integrated run is required. Do not mix earlier passing counts with later source revisions.

Those three artifacts are full runtime-revalidated proposal bindings. The actual expected pytest XML has a flat `testsuites` root; the validator also handles a single `testsuite` root. No change to the frozen scientific configuration or model/training source was found in the reviewed tracked diff. Residual limitations below are material and must remain in the proposal and handoff.

## Limits and required final stop

- Policy B explicitly permits unresolved unrelated desktop C+G/compute contexts. It does **not** establish hardware-wide exclusivity, attribute their work, stop unrelated processes, or reserve future physical free memory.
- The 4 GiB launch and 2 GiB runtime floors are conservative engineering choices. The recorded authentic forward allocator peak was 203,423,744 B reserved; the separate synthetic controller peak was 331,350,016 B. Neither measures authentic backward/context long-run requirements or proves guaranteed OOM avoidance.
- Process, lease, memory, source, and heartbeat checks remain cooperative protection under the existing trusted interpreter/dependency/operator boundary, not a hostile-admin/Python sandbox. A native/GIL stall is bounded by the external watchdog assumptions documented in the implementation.
- Full source/asset/initialization hashes, exact guarded argv, physical/logical worker profile, implementation/policy hashes, fixed recipe/limits, fresh actual-freeze expiration, and all-pass regression receipt must be frozen **after** source quiescence. Source changes after that require a new proposal, never mutated evidence.

- After producing a new immutable **UNAPPROVED** proposal, stop for the separate human decision. No authentic optimizer/training launch or fabricated approval is permitted by this review.
