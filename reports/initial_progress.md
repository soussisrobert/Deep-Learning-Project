## F. Scientific assessment

**Credible but untested.** Crop geometry establishes that source dimensions and resized footprint are not interchangeable; it does not establish a tracking failure pattern or the benefit of adaptive fusion. Pixel support is also not actual information under blur/compression/optics/occlusion. Existing attention may already compensate.

**Strong novelty challenges:** LoReTrack and ZoomTrack on resolution/sampling, SiamHSFT on hierarchy, quality-aware tracking such as TSDTrack/ATOM. Broader scale/quality-conditioned fusion remains an open literature search requirement, not a novelty gap proved by this initialization.

**Cheapest falsification:** first ask whether source support varies within comparable footprint/containment/aspect/motion/visibility strata; absent overlap makes the question unidentifiable on that sample. Then a no-training same-pair antialiased down/up-sampling intervention fixes crop/footprint/state and tests the **interaction** of degradation with prespecified shallow/deep gain perturbations. Mere performance loss after degradation is not supporting evidence. If a held-out sequence-cluster interval lies within a prespecified practically negligible interaction range, reject that local mechanism; a wide interval is inconclusive. Confirm any surviving synthetic mechanism with naturally low-support real frames. Detailed [hypothesis/preregistration draft](../docs/research_hypothesis.md) separates exploratory B0 evidence from the later corrected B2 study. **Current recommendation: no-go for substantive R1 training; go for baseline/data validation and cheap diagnostics.**
